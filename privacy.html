Privacy Policy

my swu binder

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

my swu binder is a collection management application designed for Star Wars Unlimited players and collectors.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information.

2. Information We Collect

my swu binder does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number or postal address.

3. Collection Data

Information about your card collection, including card quantities and variants, is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Internet Access

The application may use an internet connection to obtain or update card information and card images.

Your personal collection data is not transmitted as part of these requests.

5. Third-Party Services

my swu binder does not use advertising services, analytics services or third-party tracking services.

6. Data Security

Because collection information is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on the security settings of your device.

7. Children's Privacy

my swu binder does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or my swu binder, please contact us through the application's official website.
